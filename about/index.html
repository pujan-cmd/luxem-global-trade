About Luxem Global Trade

Brass, made in Jamnagar. Delivered to the world.

Since 1998, we have connected the skilled brass-parts makers of Jamnagar with buyers who need dependable components.

Luxem Global Trade was founded in 1998 in Jamnagar, the Gujarat city known as India's brass capital. From the beginning our purpose has been simple: connect the skilled brass manufacturers of Jamnagar with buyers who need dependable components, on time and to specification.

Today we supply turned, forged, cast and fabricated brass components to industrial buyers, OEMs and distributors around the world. We work from your drawing, sample or standard — managing material sourcing, production with our trusted supplier units, inspection, packing and shipping as one coordinated service.

Our measure of success is straightforward: parts that fit, paperwork that is right, and shipments that arrive when promised. That is how long-term export relationships are built.

Why Jamnagar

A whole industry, within reach.

Jamnagar is home to one of India's densest clusters of brass-part makers — foundries, forging units, CNC turning shops, platers and packers in close proximity. That ecosystem is what lets us source, iterate and ship quickly, at competitive cost.
