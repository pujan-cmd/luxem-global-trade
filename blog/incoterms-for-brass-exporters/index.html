Incoterms for Brass Component Orders: EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF and DAP Explained

17 Sept 20262 min readLuxem Global Trade export desk
← All articles

Incoterms are the International Chamber of Commerce rules that divide the tasks, costs and risks of a delivery between seller and buyer. Choosing the right one avoids disputes later. These are the five we quote most often for brass components.

TermSeller handlesRisk passes to buyer
EXW — Ex WorksGoods ready at seller's premisesWhen goods are made available at the premises
FOB — Free on BoardExport clearance and loading on the vesselWhen goods are on board at the port of shipment
CFR — Cost and FreightFOB plus sea freight to the destination portWhen goods are on board at the port of shipment
CIF — Cost, Insurance and FreightCFR plus minimum cargo insuranceWhen goods are on board at the port of shipment
DAP — Delivered at PlaceCarriage to the named place, ready for unloadingAt the named place of delivery

A common point of confusion

Under CFR and CIF the seller pays for the sea freight, but risk still passes to the buyer when the goods are loaded on the vessel at origin. Insurance under CIF is a minimum level; buyers who want wider cover often arrange their own.

Containers and the newer terms

FOB, CFR and CIF are written for goods loaded onto a ship. Because containers are normally handed to the carrier at a terminal before loading, the ICC suggests FCA, CPT or CIP for containerised cargo. Many buyers still prefer the familiar sea terms, and both approaches work if everyone is clear.

Which suits you?

  • You have a freight forwarder and want control of shipping: FOB or FCA
  • You want a landed freight price to your port: CFR or CIF
  • You want delivery to your door with import clearance on your side: DAP

Key takeaways

  • Incoterms define who arranges transport, who pays for it and where risk transfers.
  • FOB, CFR and CIF are sea-freight terms; for containers, FCA, CPT or CIP are often more precise.
  • Always name the exact port or place and the Incoterms edition, e.g. "FOB Mundra, Incoterms 2020".
