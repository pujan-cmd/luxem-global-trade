FAQ

        
Frequently asked questions.

        
Quick answers for importers, distributors and OEM buyers. Can't find what you need? Use the form above or email our export desk.

      

      
What does Luxem Global Trade export?
We export brass components from Jamnagar, India: plumbing fittings and adaptors, gas parts, hardware and sanitary parts, auto parts, forged and cast brass, inserts and fasteners, cable glands and connectors, turned parts and electrical components.
Can you manufacture brass parts to my drawing or sample?
Yes. We work build-to-print from your drawing, sample or specification, and for new parts we produce a pre-production sample for your approval before the bulk run.
Which brass grades do you supply?
Our most common grades are CW614N / C36000 (free-cutting), CW617N / C37700 (forging), CW602N (dezincification-resistant) and CW724R / C69300 (lead-free), plus C26000 for stamped parts. We confirm the grade against your drawing and end-use.
Which countries and regions do you export to?
We supply importers, distributors, wholesalers, OEM manufacturers and trading companies across Europe, North America, the Middle East, Africa and Asia-Pacific, by sea and air freight.
What Incoterms and payment terms do you offer?
We quote on EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF and DAP. Payment terms, such as T/T or L/C, are agreed per order at the quotation stage.
Do you provide material certificates and compliance documents?
Yes, on request. We can supply material test certificates (including EN 10204 3.1), inspection reports, certificates of origin, and RoHS and REACH declarations.
How do I request a quotation?
Use the enquiry form on this page, email info@luxemglobaltrade.com, or call or WhatsApp +91 85111 58598. Include your drawing or sample, brass grade, quantity, destination country and preferred Incoterm. We reply within one business day.
Where is your registered office?
Our registered office is at Faliya Wadi, B-16, Udyog Nagar, Digvijay Plot, Jamnagar, Gujarat 361005, India.
