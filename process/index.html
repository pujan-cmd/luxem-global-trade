Our process

From enquiry to delivery, nothing left to chance.

A clear, documented route for every order — so you know what happens, when, and what paperwork arrives with your cargo.

01

Enquiry & drawing review

Send drawings, samples or a specification. Our team reviews feasibility, material, tolerances and finish, and raises technical questions up front, not after production.

You receive

  • RFQ acknowledgement
  • Technical clarifications
02

Quotation

An itemised quotation with unit price, Incoterm, payment terms, packing and lead time. No hidden extras between quote and invoice.

You receive

  • Quotation
  • Proforma invoice

03

Sampling & approval

For new parts we produce pre-production samples for your approval before bulk manufacturing begins. Approved samples become the reference for inspection.

You receive

  • Pre-production samples
  • Sample inspection report
04

Material, production & finishing

Brass bar or forging stock is sourced against the specified grade, then produced with our trusted manufacturing partners in Jamnagar under in-process dimensional checks. Plating and finishing — nickel, chrome, tin or polish — are completed before inspection.

You receive

  • Material test certificate
  • Production updates
05

Inspection & testing

Final inspection against the drawing and AQL sampling plan, with thread gauging, plating checks and pressure tests where applicable.

You receive

  • Inspection report
  • Photos on request
06

Packing, documents & shipping

Export-worthy packing and marking, full shipping documentation, and freight by sea or air through established Indian gateway ports, with tracking until delivery.

You receive

  • Commercial invoice & packing list
  • Bill of lading / airway bill
  • Certificate of origin

Packing & protection

Packed to arrive exactly as it left.

Brass is robust, but threads and finished surfaces are not. We pack to protect them through handling, containerisation and ocean transit.

Parts are bagged by lot or size, interleaved where needed, cartoned and palletised or crated. Anti-tarnish packing and moisture protection are available for plated and polished components. Every carton is marked with part number, quantity and lot.
